# KOVIAN Brand Identity

The KOVIAN ecosystem uses one shared symbol across every product. Do not create a separate logo mark for individual products.

- **KOVIAN** — master brand and ecosystem.
- **Kovian Finance** — shared KOVIAN symbol with the Finance product name.
- **Kovian Fitness** — shared KOVIAN symbol with the Fitness product name.
- **Kovi AI** — shared KOVIAN symbol with the AI product name.

## Visual rules

- Use the shared KOVIAN artwork in frontend public assets (or the product's identical SVG copy) for app icons and brand marks.
- Keep the charcoal, white, emerald, cyan, and blue palette.
- Product names may vary; the KOVIAN symbol must not.
- Do not stretch, recolor, redraw, or add a different product-specific symbol.
